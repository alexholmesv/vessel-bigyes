{# Rights explanation #}

As a student enrolled on the BigYes platform, you benefit from the following rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • — Right of Access (Art. 15). You may request confirmation of whether we process personal data about you and obtain a copy of it.
  • — Right to Data Portability (Art. 20). You may receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format and transmit it to another controller.
  • — Right to Erasure (Art. 17). You may request the permanent deletion of all personal data we hold about you, subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • — Right to Rectification (Art. 16). You may correct inaccurate personal data at any time via your account settings.

For questions about how your data is used, contact us at privacy@bigyes.local .
